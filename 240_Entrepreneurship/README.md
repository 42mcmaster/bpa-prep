# 240 Entrepreneurship

**Format:** Write a full business plan for a new venture, then give a 5-7 minute investor pitch to judges plus 5 minutes of questions. Must do both parts to be ranked.
**Reference materials in the real test:** Not a test. You build the project ahead of time and submit it online, then (for the judged events) present it to judges at the competition.

**Deadline:** Submit the business plan by **April 1, 2027** as one PDF: title page, table of contents, plan, works cited, release forms, AI form.
**AI policy:** Allowed with citation (Level 2).
