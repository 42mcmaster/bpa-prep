# 365 Website Design and Development Team

**Format:** Team of 2-4 builds a website for "Marlowby," a monthly subscription box featuring a different U.S. city. Needs a homepage, subscription plans, featured cities, local businesses, sign-up call to action, and an about page. Then a 10-minute presentation to judges plus 5 minutes of questions.
**Reference materials in the real test:** Not a test. You build the project ahead of time and submit it online, then (for the judged events) present it to judges at the competition.

**Deadline:** Submit by **April 1, 2027** as one PDF with a clickable URL to the site, works cited, release forms, and the AI form.
**AI policy:** Allowed with citation (Level 2).
Needs 2-4 team members. Bring the site on your own laptop; no projector is provided.
