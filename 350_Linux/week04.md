# BPA 350 Linux — Week 04: Hands-On Lab on the Raspberry Pi

## Overview
Type the commands the real test asks about, on a real Linux computer, and answer from what the screen shows. You take turns at the Raspberry Pi. The Form grades itself.

**Time Estimate:** 30-40 minutes at the Pi, then Gimkit

**What you need:**
- [350 Linux Reference Guide](350_Linux_Reference_Guide.md), printed — you will need Section 26
- A Chromebook or lab PC next to the Pi for the Form

---

## Part 1: Get Into Your Folder

1. On the Pi, open the terminal (the black window icon at the top of the screen).
2. Type this, using **your own first name**, and press Enter:

   ```
   cd ~/bpa_lab/cody
   ```

3. Stay in that folder the whole time.

If you see "No such file or directory," check the spelling of your name.

---

## Part 2: The Form (graded)

**Open the Form:** FORM LINK HERE

- 22 questions, 2 points each.
- Questions 1-17 tell you exactly what to type. Type it on the Pi, read the screen, answer in the Form.
- Questions 18-22 only tell you what to find out. Use the Quick Reference Card (Section 26) to find the command.
- A few questions change your folder (permissions, extracting an archive). That is expected.
- If you break your folder, tell Mr. McMaster. It resets in ten seconds.

**Stuck?**
- Everything says "No such file": you are in the wrong folder. Do Part 1 again.
- The screen fills with text and you cannot get out: press `q`.
- A command seems frozen: press Ctrl+C.
