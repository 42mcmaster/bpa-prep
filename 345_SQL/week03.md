# BPA 345 SQL — Week 03: Write the Questions

## Overview
This week you write the Gimkit instead of playing it. Pick **one section** of the study guide and write **10 multiple-choice questions** from it. The best questions become the class Gimkit next week.

Writing a good question, especially believable wrong answers, makes you understand the material better than answering does.

**Time Estimate:** 40-45 minutes, then replay an earlier Gimkit

**What you need:**
- [345 SQL Reference Guide](345_SQL_Reference_Guide.md)
- The Form link below

---

## Part 1: Pick a Section
Choose one section of the guide. Pick one you are weakest on. If several students are doing this week, spread out so you do not all pick the same section.

---

## Part 2: Write 10 Questions (graded)

**Open the Form:** FORM LINK HERE

For each question you enter: the question, the correct answer, and three wrong answers.

**Rules:**
1. Every correct answer must be something the guide says in your section.
2. Wrong answers must be **believable**: real SQL keywords, real clauses, or real functions that do a nearby job. No joke answers.
3. No true/false. No "all of the above" or "none of the above."
4. Do not copy a sentence from the guide word for word. Turn it into a question.
5. Keep it short: question under 120 characters, answers under 60.
6. Mix the kinds of question. Include at least two predict-the-output questions against the School tables in Section 3.

**Example of a good question:**
> **Question:** Which clause filters groups AFTER aggregation?
> **Correct:** `HAVING`
> **Wrong:** `WHERE` · `GROUP BY` · `ORDER BY`
> (All three wrong answers are real SQL keywords that do a nearby job. That is what makes it a good question.)

You can come back and edit your answers after submitting.
