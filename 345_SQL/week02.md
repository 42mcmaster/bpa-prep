# BPA 345 SQL — Week 02: Timed Mini-Test, Two Rounds

## Overview
A practice test in two rounds. **Round 1 is closed book and timed**, like the real competition. **Round 2 is the same questions, open book**, and that is your grade. The point is to find out what you know from memory, then fix the gaps using the guide.

**Time Estimate:** 30 minutes for Round 1, 20 minutes for Round 2, then Gimkit

**What you need:**
- [345 SQL Reference Guide](345_SQL_Reference_Guide.md) — put it away for Round 1, open it for Round 2
- The two Form links below
- The School tables from Section 3 are printed on every page of both Forms
