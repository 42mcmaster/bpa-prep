# 420 Digital Media Production

**Format:** Individual. Make a 1-2 minute informational video on how AI is used in everyday life and how to use it responsibly, for a middle and high school audience. Then a 10-minute presentation to judges plus 5 minutes of questions.
**Reference materials in the real test:** Not a test. You build the project ahead of time and submit it online, then (for the judged events) present it to judges at the competition.

**Deadline:** Submit by **April 1, 2027** as one PDF with a link to the video, works cited, release forms, and the AI form.
**AI policy:** **Not allowed** (Level 1), even though the topic is AI.
