# 450 Podcast Production Team

**Format:** Team of 2-4 records a 3-5 minute podcast: "Myth vs. Reality: Teenagers and Technology." Must cover two required myths (teens are addicted to phones; technology only distracts and harms learning) with real evidence. Then a 10-minute presentation to judges plus 5 minutes of questions.
**Reference materials in the real test:** Not a test. You build the project ahead of time and submit it online, then (for the judged events) present it to judges at the competition.

**Deadline:** Submit by **April 1, 2027** as one PDF with a link to the podcast, works cited, release forms, and the AI form.
**AI policy:** **Not allowed** (Level 1).
